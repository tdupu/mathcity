# Product follow-up

## P3: failed expectation

a=1 and b=2 give ab=2 even, but a is odd. Thus P1 is false.

## P4: corrected statement

For integers a,b, the product ab is even if and only if at least one factor is
even. An even factor supplies a factor of 2. If both factors are odd, write
a=2r+1 and b=2s+1; then ab=2(2rs+r+s)+1 is odd. This proves the converse by
contraposition, using the even/odd dichotomy.

The counterexample to P1 remains relevant even after P4 replaces it.
