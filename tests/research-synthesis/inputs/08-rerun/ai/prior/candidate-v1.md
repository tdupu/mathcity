# Interval squares

The notation [0,1] denotes the real numbers x satisfying 0<=x<=1.
For a real number x in [0,1], x squared<=x. Both x and 1-x are nonnegative,
so x(1-x)=x-x squared>=0. Equality occurs exactly at x=0 or x=1, by the
zero-product property of real numbers. For example, x=1/2 gives 1/4<=1/2.

Sources: bounds-v1.md, B1 and B2. Assumed background: real order, products
of nonnegative numbers, the zero-product property, and elementary algebra.
