# Square bounds

Version: R1.

## B1: interval bound

For every real number x with 0<=x<=1, x squared<=x. Indeed x>=0 and 1-x>=0,
so x(1-x)>=0, hence x-x squared>=0.

## B2: equality

Under the same hypotheses, equality holds exactly when x=0 or x=1, since
x-x squared=x(1-x) is zero precisely at those two values.
