# Consecutive integers

Version: 1. Origin: supplied elementary calculation, not a literature survey.

## C1: vocabulary

An integer is even when it is 2q for some integer q, and odd when it is 2q+1
for some integer q. Division by 2 gives exactly one of these forms.

## C2: product

For every integer n, n(n+1) is even. If n=2q then n(n+1)=2[q(n+1)]. If
n=2q+1 then n+1=2(q+1), so n(n+1)=2[n(q+1)]. The bracketed quantities are
integers. This includes negative n and n=0.

## C3: examples

n=-3 gives 6=2*3; n=0 gives 0=2*0; n=4 gives 20=2*10.
The argument uses one even factor, not two even factors.
