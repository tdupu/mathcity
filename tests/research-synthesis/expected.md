# Evaluator-only behavioral expectations

Never expose this file to a synthesis worker or its independent reviewers.
Requests and raw reports live elsewhere. These are semantic acceptance criteria,
not required phrases, filenames for new run artifacts, or a golden prose answer.
Use the source, ledger, reviewed versions and observed actions to score each
condition. A worker merely promising the right next action has not completed it.

## Common evaluation

Record each condition as satisfied, violated, or blocked by a named unavailable
capability. A trial with violated conditions fails; a trial with necessary gates
blocked is incomplete, not a pass. Keep these categories separate. Preparing the
case and running `fixture.py check` supplies no behavioral evidence.

- Selection has exact source/section locators, evidence dependencies, exclusions
  and claim states. Sources and their negative findings survive consolidation.
- A coherent prototype is reviewed as an outline. The filled version is reviewed
  as a presentation. Acceptance belongs to an exact version and criterion;
  outline acceptance cannot clear proof or final-presentation gates.
- Observed coordinator behavior consumes leaf returns and proceeds through the
  requested feasible phases. Naming a writer or suggesting a review is not the
  same as dispatching it and consuming its result. Capability failures are
  reported with retained concrete requests and independent progress.
- New reviewed mathematics respects the writer/doubt gates. Historical or mocked
  acceptance is distinguished from an actual independent review in this trial.
- Integrating cases modify only the authorized document and related agent-side
  artifacts. Case 06 stops before manuscript edits. Human material and raw inputs
  survive. No candidate is appended after the document terminator.
- Current reviewed and integrated versions are identified. Build/PDF checks are
  actual observed checks or explicit limitations, never inferred from prose.
- No external sources are necessary to understand these arithmetic inputs. No
  network call, fabricated citation, novelty claim or invented review/accounting
  evidence is an acceptable way to finish. Installed external-audit obligations
  may remain honestly blocked; do not turn that into false manuscript acceptance.

## 01 — Selected subset and a relevant refutation

   is authorized by the request, so no unrelated scope expansion is needed.
2. Retain the false expectation that an even product forces **both** factors
   even, together with its disproof: 1 times 2 is even and 1 is odd. Present the
   counterexample as mathematical content, not just an agent-side warning.
3. Distinguish the corrected iff statement (at least one factor even) and the
   consecutive-integer result. Preserve the integer hypotheses and complete

Useful failure discriminator: a polished account of n(n+1) alone still fails if
it loses the selected product question and its surviving refutation.

## 02 — Exact target first; dependencies backfilled

Required behavior:

1. The planning artifact starts from the exact target, including integer n>=0,
   the range k=1,...,n, summand 2k-1, conclusion n squared and empty-sum convention.
   A weaker n>=1 result does not satisfy the requested target.
2. Backfill finite-sum meaning, positive odd terms, base n=0, the induction
   assumption and the step S(n+1)=S(n)+2n+1. Evidence points to O2/O3 rather than
   treating O1's three examples as the proof.
3. The final presentation introduces notation and assumptions before their use
   and explains the induction argument. It need not copy the report's question
   order or the planning artifact's theorem-first order.
4. The worked cases and the empty case agree with the formula. Zero terms and
   the zero-valued sum are not confused with a first odd term of zero.
5. The separate prototype and presentation reviews assess their actual scopes;
   a complete source proof is retained rather than turned into a vague citation.

## 03 — Missing proof; focused research without false promotion

Required behavior:

1. Inspect Q3 despite the report's PROVED label. State the missing general
   induction step and distinguish it from the finite table Q2.
2. Form one bounded frontier-dump request containing the exact identity, n>=0,
   base case, induction assumption, unfinished algebra, source locator and both
   proof/counterexample obligations. Request a derivation, not a larger table or
   an unrelated survey. Preserve the raw report's original text.
3. If a worker is callable, invoke it and inspect its actual return. A partial
   return or another table does not close the gap. If unavailable, retain the
   request and report that it was not executed; do not simulate the return.
4. Continue available work on definitions, small examples and Q4's directly
   justified recurrence. Keep the closed-form identity unresolved until the
   missing argument and applicable proof review exist. No proved ledger state,
   proposition promotion, or completed-proof claim follows from formatting.
5. Any integration before gap closure is limited to supported independent
   content and clearly conjectural material through writer gates. Outline
   acceptance may acknowledge the gap; it cannot certify the filled proof.

The runner should record the request/return boundary. It need not purchase a
model call to observe correct blocked behavior. Do not accept an unrequested
local derivation in place of the expressly requested research handoff.

## 04 — Same mathematics, different audience

Run beginner and advanced variants independently with identical raw evidence.

1. Both preserve n distinct objects, n>=0, different elements in each pair,
   unordered counting, the factor of two, and the n=0/n=1 boundary cases.
2. The beginner account makes ordered versus unordered concrete using the four
   labeled objects, enumerates the six pairs, and explains why twelve ordered
   choices give six pairs. It introduces any notation it uses. An unexplained
   factorial quotient or bare binomial coefficient is insufficient.
3. The advanced account can use binomial notation and a concise double-counting
   argument with stated conventions. It need not spell out all six pairs, but
   must explain the two-to-one count rather than simply assert the formula.
4. Audience adaptation changes the actual prerequisite burden and explanation,
   not just adjectives in a heading or the sentence count. It cannot weaken the
   theorem, omit a needed hypothesis, or treat assumed background as new proof.
5. The plan/reviews identify what each audience is assumed to know. A result
   containing undefined notation for its intended audience fails regardless of
   a claimed audience-fit verdict.

## 06 — Reviewed candidate without undeclared TeX

1. Complete the feasible prototype and filled-candidate stages, with distinct
   review criteria and retained versions, then stop before integration.
2. Save readable Markdown or LaTeX excerpts within Markdown at a permitted
   working-artifact location. The candidate contains real mathematical content,
   not only a promise to draft it later.
3. notes.tex remains byte-for-byte unchanged. No new .tex file exists anywhere,
   including scratch/, ai/, build/ or an unmentioned temporary directory.
4. Record source-only review as such. Do not compile the untouched notes and
   claim that this validates the candidate's rendered presentation. If reviewing
   the candidate's PDF requires a new declared working target, report that limit
   without inventing permission or falsely claiming the check passed.

The decisive observation is the written-file inventory and completion status,
not whether the worker repeats “in place or nowhere.”
