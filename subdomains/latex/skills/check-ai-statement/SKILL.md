Read and follow `latex-ai-statement` ([canonical skill](../latex-ai-statement/SKILL.md)).
Pass the target `.tex` file, the resolved policy, and any active workflow
unchanged. The canonical audit also checks the statement's own provenance: it
must explicitly say that the statement was generated, drafted, or written by
AI; identify the model, harness, and date; name the skills or workflows
actually used (distinguishing executed from merely consulted); and distinguish
AI drafting from human review and responsibility. Do not start a second audit
or maintain a separate check table here.
