# UVM sources

Fetch current pages each invocation; follow their links for the requested term. Record the exact section/CRN, source URL, access date and resolved facts. These links are starting points, not a frozen policy snapshot.

| Question | Primary source |
|---|---|
| Current syllabus template and guidance | [CTL templates](https://www.uvm.edu/ctl/syllabus-templates), [syllabus resources](https://www.uvm.edu/ctl/syllabus-resources) |
| Authoritative university policies | [Policy index](https://www.uvm.edu/policies/z-policy-index) and the current policies it links |
| Meeting pattern, room, credits, instructor | [Registrar schedule of courses](https://www.uvm.edu/registrar/schedule-courses), then the term's section listing |
| Breaks, first/last class, reading days | [Academic calendar](https://www.uvm.edu/registrar/uvm-academic-calendar) |
| Assigned final date, start/end, room | [Final exams](https://www.uvm.edu/registrar/final-exams), then the term's course/CRN assignments |

Read the linked template document, not only its landing page. Check applicable college/department requirements too. Classify each template item as required, conditional, or recommended using its wording and linked policies; suggestions are not mandates. Use current policy links and required wording where specified. Include conditional disclosures only when the course uses the relevant platform, recording, modality or assessment tool. Do not carry obsolete health statements or religious-observance deadlines forward.

Keep institutional requirements separate from instructor choices. Verify objectives, grading, materials, contact/office hours, attendance, integrity/AI, accommodations and any applicable emergency, evaluation or curriculum-designation statements. Resolve applicability rather than silently omitting requirements for brevity.

The [fall exam matrix](https://www.uvm.edu/registrar/fall-final-exam-matrix) is a guideline, not a section assignment. Match the official assignment by term and CRN; do not substitute another section's time. Record the institution's local time zone. An unpublished/inaccessible assignment stays unverified; deliver a labeled draft and the exact missing fact. Official scheduling resolves institutional dates, not the instructor's grading or final-activity policy.
