# Generate a syllabus

Keep the student document minimal; keep research and unresolved decisions outside it.

1. Read applicable repository instructions and course policies. Resolve the course, term, section/CRN, canonical syllabus, old webpage and its linked syllabus, current webpage, accepted decisions, and build conventions. Find available files before asking for paths. Prior offerings are baselines, not current authority.
2. Verify current institutional syllabus requirements, academic calendar, official section meeting details, and assigned final slot for the selected offering. For UVM, read [the source guide](references/uvm-sources.md). Do not extrapolate from an old term or a generic exam matrix. Record URLs and access dates in the working review, not repetitive student prose.
3. Make a compact source-linked review table: item, current evidence, proposed value, status (confirmed/historical/unresolved). Cover logistics, objectives/materials, grading, assessments, attendance/missed work, redos, collaboration/AI, accessibility, required statements and links. Surface conflicts across syllabus, policies, accepted ADRs, and webpages; filename recency does not settle them. An assigned final slot does not establish whether it hosts an exam, redos, or another activity. Distinguish design-time targets from student time allowances.
4. Reuse confirmed choices. Ask only about missing consequential decisions or invalidated approvals; draft supported sections meanwhile. Preserve the adopted syllabus until replacement is approved. Never silently change student-facing grading or assessment rules. Record approved substantive decisions once using the repository's decision convention.
5. Adapt the existing template. Include required content and concise course-specific rules; link optional institutional resources where permitted. Do not reproduce the entire university template merely because it exists. Keep detailed schedules on the course page unless required in the syllabus. Label unresolved draft content; never invent facts. Present the complete draft and exact changes for instructor approval before adoption.
6. Check grading arithmetic, dates, assessment counts, policy consistency, links and placeholders. Build and visually inspect the requested output with local tools; use `using-latex` for LaTeX within the existing plan. Keep private assessments, solutions and student data out. Report source/rendered paths, checks, approval state and remaining decisions. Generation alone does not authorize publication.

Reconcile rather than rewrite. Preserve canonical paths, confirmations and decision IDs. With unchanged inputs and verified outputs, report a no-op; do not refresh dates, duplicate records or overwrite PDFs for build timestamps. Rebuild only changed/missing/stale outputs or for validation; use temporary output for validation-only rebuilds. New offerings require fresh term-specific verification. Honor requested prose revisions even when facts are unchanged.
